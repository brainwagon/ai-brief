  • Reading Is Not Using: Retrieval, Judgment, and the Design of AI Financial Research Workflows

    The paper identifies a retrieval-integration gap in LLMs used as financial analysts, showing that retrieved information often fails to influence investment judgments despite accurate retrieval, and demonstrates that workflow architecture (e.g., targeted restatement vs. chunk-and-summarize) critically determines whether retrieved disclosures affect decisions.

    Score 4cs.CL, cs.AI · submitted 25 Aug

  • Effective Learning Rate Governs Loss Dynamics in Language Model Pretraining

    The paper identifies the effective learning rate (learning rate divided by parameter norm) as a unified coordinate governing loss dynamics in language model pretraining, showing that matching ELR across different training setups collapses loss trajectories and enables transfer of scaling laws, with ablations revealing normalization design and LR-norm variation timescale as key factors.

    Score 4cs.LG · submitted 25 Aug

  • Strictly Causal Streaming Video Anomaly Detection with a Theoretically-Grounded State-Space Core

  • EMFE: A lightweight, explainable machine learning framework for malaria cell classification

    EMFE is a lightweight, explainable ML framework using five classical features (color normalization, thresholding, morphological detection) with Random Forest to classify malaria-infected blood cells, achieving 94.6% accuracy on patient-grouped cross-validation and outperforming deep models in efficiency and interpretability.

    Score 4cs.CV · submitted 25 Aug

  • StepGuard: Learning Step-Level Guardrails with Scalable Supervision and Safety-Utility Balancing

    StepGuard is a step-level guard model that audits agent trajectories and checks tool actions before execution, trained via StepGen data generation and Balance-GRPO to balance safety and utility, reducing attack success by 77.3% with minimal utility loss.

    Score 4cs.AI · submitted 25 Aug

  • Evidence Blindness in Direct Corpus Interaction: Persistent Navigation with AtlasNav

    AtlasNav introduces a persistent multi-view corpus navigation framework that builds a reusable Corpus Atlas once, enabling adaptive query navigation without reconstructing shared structure per query; on BrowseComp-Plus it achieves 92.05% strict accuracy while reducing online inference cost by 30.21% versus prior dynamic-workspace methods.

    Score 4cs.AI · submitted 25 Aug

  • ExpConCAD: Experience-Guided Text-to-CAD Generation from Shape Descriptions with Implicit Spatial Constraints

    ExpConCAD generates executable CAD programs from natural language by inferring missing spatial constraints through construction structure analysis and retrieval of reusable design experience, producing CadQuery code from underspecified descriptions.

  • Do Robotic World Models Really Follow Actions? Diagnosing and Aligning Action-Conditioned Generation for Policy Learning

    The paper introduces WorldEcho to evaluate how well action-conditioned world models follow arbitrary robot actions, finds they fail on off-expert actions, and proposes WorldSync to improve action following via distributional coverage, representational grounding, and intervention alignment, showing better policy improvement in simulations and real-robot tasks.
