  • Let the Fixer Decide: Training a Code Localizer from the Fixer's Likelihood

    A method called Fixer-Guided Localization (FGL) trains a 4B-parameter code localizer by having a frozen fixer score proposed localizations on how likely they produce a verified patch, then using those scores as training targets via weighted supervised fine-tuning, without tests or reinforcement learning; on the Kaggle Gemma-4 Developer Agent competition's development set, the approach increases solved tasks to 10.6% from 8.7% and reduces agent loops that read nonexistent files.

    PickScore 443 upvotes · arXiv 2611.12164

  • Learning Video Foresight Reasoning from Unlabeled Videos via On-Policy Self-Distillation

    Future-OPSD is an on-policy self-distillation framework that learns video foresight reasoning from unlabeled past-future video pairs, using a foresight score and contrastive privilege advantage to amplify decision-relevant future information. It improves base vision-language models by up to 14.2 points on open-ended prediction and 15.9 on closed-ended planning without extra annotations or LLM supervision.

    Score 437 upvotes · arXiv 2611.29338

  • ViDecide: Teaching Video Diffusion Models to Reason and Plan Step by Step

    ViDecide trains video diffusion models to predict causally ordered state transitions and decide when to stop, using a decision-focused training schedule and a corrective fine-tuning method that maps failed decisions to the model's own successful continuations; it achieves 83.8% average strict success rate on abstract games, planning tasks, and multi-step robot planning benchmarks, outperforming standard fine-tuning (23.3%) and the strongest prior method (67.8%).
