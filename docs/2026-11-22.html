  • Posterior Matching Distillation

    Posterior Matching Distillation (PMD) is a distribution-based diffusion distillation method that matches exponentially reweighted averages of student samples to teacher posterior means, recovering the teacher's full posterior without an auxiliary network, achieving best FID among 4-step methods on SDXL and FLUX.2 [klein] 4B.

    PickScore 4cs.CV · submitted 19 Nov

  • RL Post-Training in Diffusion Transformers Is Activation Steering

    A study shows that a fixed set of steering vectors added to the frozen base diffusion transformer, with 65× fewer parameters than a LoRA, recovers 90–96% of the reward gains from RL post-training in image and video models, and can even exceed weight-based RL on specific metrics.

    PickScore 4cs.CV, cs.LG · submitted 19 Nov

  • PlayCraft: Designing Engaging Games from Agent Play Experience

    PlayCraft is an automated framework that uses a vision-based agent to play games and generate detailed reports, which a crafter agent then uses to revise the games for improved player mastery and engagement, showing larger learning gains and steeper learning slopes than initial games and baselines across three intelligence settings.

    Score 4cs.AI, cs.CV · submitted 19 Nov

  • TideCache: Efficient Hybrid LLM Serving with Transparent Cache Management and Layer-Wise Streaming

    TideCache is a serving system for hybrid LLMs on CPU-GPU superchips that transparently manages GPU memory among heterogeneous caches via a transparent cache pool, streams prefetched global-attention layer prefixes using computation intervals from bounded-state layers, and schedules requests adaptively; implemented in SGLang on GB300 and GH200, it completes up to 2.04x more agent turns and reduces P50 per-token latency by 20-59% over SGLang with HiCache.

    Score 4cs.DC, cs.LG · submitted 19 Nov

  • One Prior, Two Dynamics: Repurposing Video Diffusion Transformers for Self-Supervised Latent Action Learning

    O2LaF initializes both an inverse dynamics model and a forward dynamics model from the same pretrained video diffusion transformer, learning latent actions from unlabeled videos via a compact bottleneck. It sets state-of-the-art few-shot action probing and enables zero-shot action transfer across visual contexts and embodiments in navigation and robotic manipulation.

    Score 4cs.CV, cs.AI · submitted 19 Nov

  • ScaleWorld: High-Resolution World Generation through Shared Prediction and Refinement

    ScaleWorld uses a single shared causal video model to perform low-resolution world prediction and one-step high-resolution refinement, streaming generation with separate causal states and task-asymmetric on-policy distillation.

    Score 4cs.CV · submitted 19 Nov

  • Bio-PM-Guided Synthetic IMU Generation

    This work uses a pretrained biosignal encoder (Bio-PM) to convert 10-second IMU windows into token embeddings, then trains class-conditional autoregressive transformer and diffusion decoders to generate synthetic IMU tokens. On the WISDM benchmark, combining synthetic-token augmentation with a 320-dimensional gravity-aware feature representation improves leave-one-subject-out macro-F1 from 0.729 to 0.809 across 51 subjects.

    Score 4cs.LG · submitted 19 Nov

  • Depth Pro 2: Sharp and Temporally Consistent Metric Monocular Depth

    A scalable monocular depth estimation network that jointly processes single images and video to produce dense metric depth maps at native resolution, achieving simultaneously high boundary sharpness, temporal consistency, and metric accuracy; a family of models is released establishing a new Pareto frontier across accuracy, sharpness, consistency, and runtime.

    PickScore 4cs.CV, cs.LG · submitted 19 Nov
