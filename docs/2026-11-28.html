  • Damped Atomic Wave Integration Neurons (DAWIN): A Universal Primitive for Continuous-Time Spatiotemporal Learning

    DAWIN replaces standard neural layers with a continuous-time primitive that couples spatial projection and elapsed-time-dependent wave excitation via closed-form damped evolution, achieving state-of-the-art results on irregular time-series tasks including oscillators, dynamics, trajectories, and clinical sequences with parallel prefix-scan implementation.

    PickScore 41 upvotes · arXiv 2611.30025

  • BiSAT: Bidirectionally Aligned Semantic-Acoustic Tokenization for Speech Language Models

    BiSAT is a unified tokenization framework for speech language models that uses separate acoustic and semantic branches aligned by a shared-index mechanism, enabling joint optimization of both encoders without fixed semantic teacher targets, and it supports a zero-shot text-to-speech system (BiSAT-TTS) that performs comparably to state-of-the-art baselines with less training data.
