  • Ambient LLMs: Data-Efficient Supervised Fine-tuning via Temperature Matching

    Ambient SFT augments supervised fine-tuning by requiring the fine-tuned model's high-temperature predictions to match those of the frozen pre-trained model, preventing overfitting on small post-training datasets and achieving up to 21% relative improvement over standard SFT in domain adaptation, instruction-tuning, and reasoning.

    PickScore 4cs.LG, cs.AI · submitted 17 Nov

  • Spool: Low-Latency Streaming Inference for Hybrid Vision-Language Models

    Spool is a training-free framework that enables low-latency streaming inference for hybrid vision-language models by caching per-frame state transformations and composing them associatively, achieving a 6.6× reduction in p95 time-to-first-token across five hybrid VLMs and four video reasoning benchmarks.

    PickScore 4cs.CV, cs.LG · submitted 17 Nov

  • What Does a Bias Head Measure? Non-Identifiability in Head-Level Bias Attribution

    An audit of bias-attribution methods that claim to localise social bias to individual attention heads in LLMs finds that the head-level bias statistic is confounded with response length and prompt identity: on Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, the statistic is better explained by prompt identity (64% of variance) than by fairness labels, and ablation of the identified heads does not reduce unfairness.

    Score 4cs.CL, cs.LG · submitted 17 Nov

  • EviSlide-MIL: Multi-Query Pooling and Shared Prediction for Class-Incremental Whole-Slide Image Classification

    EviSlide-MIL is a class-incremental multiple instance learning framework for whole-slide image classification that uses multiple learnable queries per class and a shared prediction module to aggregate frozen patch features and limit parameter growth, combining experience replay and knowledge distillation to reduce forgetting when learning new tumor subtypes.

  • Multimodal Masked Diffusion Autoencoder for Modeling Impaired Driving

    Score 4cs.CV, cs.LG · submitted 17 Nov

  • Learning Grounded Reasoning from Human Gaze
