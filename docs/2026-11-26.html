  • LuxAvatar: Real-Time Relightable Head Avatars via Causal Neural Shading of 3D Intrinsic Proxies

    LuxAvatar is a feed-forward framework that factorizes real-time head avatar animation and relighting into an animatable 3D intrinsic Gaussian proxy and a two-step causal autoregressive neural shader, achieving 3D-controllable, real-time portrait synthesis under target illumination from a single input image.

    Score 4cs.CV · submitted 25 Nov

  • DynMF2: Rigid Motion Factorization by Expectation-Maximization for Monocular 4D Reconstruction and Motion Segmentation

    DynMF2 reconstructs a dynamic 3D scene from a single monocular video by factorizing every observed 3D point trajectory into a single rigid SE(3) motion, using Expectation-Maximization with closed-form updates and interactive tools (requery, merge, split) to determine the number of motions automatically, outperforming mask-based methods in view synthesis and 3D tracking without requiring any segmentation masks.

    Score 4cs.CV · submitted 25 Nov

  • Diverse Alternative Reasoning Traces for Hard Math Problems

    DART (Diverse Alternative Reasoning Traces) recovers 37% more hard math problems than independent Monte Carlo sampling by decomposing rejected reasoning traces into steps, ranking them by uncertainty, and generating alternative candidates with reference-answer guidance and a meta-thought, achieving a 30% lower cost per accepted trace and improving Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct's post-training performance on MATH-500 and AIME.

    Score 4cs.CL, cs.AI · submitted 25 Nov
