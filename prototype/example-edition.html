arXiv 5 Items

  • Synthetic-only instruction tuning closes the gap to Llama-3-8B at 3B parameters

    Trains a 3B base model on nothing but model-generated dialogue and reports MT-Bench parity with Llama-3-8B-Instruct, with the gain traced to diversity of instruction form rather than volume. The ablation showing collapse past four generations of self-distillation is the part worth reading.

    PickScore 5cs.CL · submitted 16 Aug

  • A closed-form bound on retrieval error under context-window truncation

    Gives an actual bound, not a benchmark, on how much recall you lose when a retrieved passage is cut at the window edge, in terms of chunk overlap and passage length. Short, and it makes chunking an engineering decision with a number attached instead of folklore.

    PickScore 5cs.CL, cs.LG · submitted 15 Aug

  • Sparse autoencoders find fewer interpretable features than reported when the dictionary is held fixed

    A replication that keeps dictionary size constant across model scales and finds most of the claimed feature growth was capacity, not structure. Measured in tone, and does not claim the whole line of work is wrong.

    Score 4cs.LG · submitted 16 Aug

  • Depth-anything distillation for monocular metric depth on embedded GPUs

    Gets metric depth at 30 fps on a Jetson Orin Nano by distilling into a 12M-parameter student, losing about 8% on absolute relative error. Relevant if the camera is on a robot rather than a server.

    Score 3cs.CV · submitted 15 Aug

  • On the identifiability of mixture weights in variational posterior collapse

    Theory-first, and narrow: conditions under which the mixture weights stay identifiable as the KL term dominates. Included because the proof technique is reusable, not because the result is.

    Score 3stat.ML · submitted 16 Aug

Hacker News 4 Items

  • The kernel scheduler change that made our p99 worse for four years

    A careful, unhurried postmortem of a latency regression that survived three kernel upgrades because every benchmark the team ran measured throughput. The bisect narrative is the good part; the fix is one sysctl.

    PickScore 5612 points · 208 comments

  • Show HN: A single-file Python profiler that survives fork

    One file, stdlib only, and it keeps sampling across fork() without the child losing its stack. Author is honest about the platform limits — Linux only, and it lies under gVisor.

    Score 4318 points · 74 comments

  • Ask HN: What broke when you moved inference off the cloud?

    Thread rather than article, and unusually free of vendor noise: repeated reports that the hard part was not the GPU but model versioning and the absence of a rollback story.

    Score 4241 points · 393 comments

  • SQLite's new JSONB format, benchmarked against the old one

    Independent numbers rather than the release notes'. Roughly 2× on reads for documents over a few kilobytes, and no measurable difference below that.

    Score 3189 points · 51 comments

Hugging Face papers 3 Items

  • Attention sinks are a byproduct of softmax, not of training

    Argues the sink token is forced by softmax needing somewhere to put probability mass, and shows it disappears with a normalisation that permits abstention. Clean result with an obvious implication for quantisation.

    Score 4184 upvotes · arXiv 2508.11866

  • Small models can be good judges if the rubric is short

    Judge agreement with humans holds up at 7B as long as the scoring rubric fits in a few hundred tokens; longer rubrics collapse it. Directly relevant to anything scoring items at home.

    Score 4121 upvotes · arXiv 2508.11512

  • A survey of speculative decoding, one year on

    Survey, so no new result, but it is the first one that puts the tree-based and draft-model families in the same notation. Useful as a map, not as reading.

    Score 376 upvotes · arXiv 2508.10788
